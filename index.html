Our story

Neighbors helping neighbors in Aresa.

ARESA Charity Group is a community organization established in Aresa in September 2015. Since then, we have brought people together to support families and individuals facing poverty or hardship.

We believe neighbors can make a meaningful difference by looking out for one another. Through community contributions, we have helped many people in Aresa.

Ramadan giving

Each Ramadan, we collect donations to share food and dates with families and local institutions in Aresa.

Ramadan campaign impact

50

families supported

Our community has raised up to 300,000 in Ramadan contributions. We provided 50 families with food packages containing 25 kg of rice, flour, dates, and cooking oil. We also donated dates to four mosques and Aresa Girls High School.

To find out how to join the WhatsApp group and contribute, please contact a current group member.
